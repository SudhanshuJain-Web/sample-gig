Safety Practices & 24/7 Field Hub (सुरक्षा नियम)

Road Accident Prevention, Night Shift Protocols & Instant SOS

Daily Pre-Shift & Field Safety Checklist

Complete this quick check before logging into delivery apps to ensure your life and vehicle safety.

1. ISI-Certified Helmet & Reflective Vest (हेलमेट व चमकीली जैकेट)

Always strap your helmet securely. Reflective vests make you visible to heavy trucks and speeding cars at night.

2. Brake & Tire Pressure Inspection (ब्रेक और टायर की जांच)

Ensure front and rear brakes bite firmly. Check tread depth to avoid skidding on wet roads and oil spills.

3. Secure Smartphone Handlebar Mount (फोन होल्डर)

Never hold phone in one hand while driving. Keep eyes on the road and rely on audio GPS guidance.

4. Extreme Weather & Heatwave Hydration (गर्मी व बारिश में पानी)

Drink ORS / electrolyte water every 45 minutes during 40°C+ summer heat to prevent heat stroke and dizziness.

5. Hostile Customer & Dark Alley Drop-off Protocol (असुरक्षित स्थान नियम)

If drop location is unlit or customer acts aggressive, step back to main gate and call platform emergency safety line.
